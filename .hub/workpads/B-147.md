## Source-backed row decisions

The [W3C SKOS Reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/) S19–S22 and
S39–S42 make every `skos:semanticRelation` descendant a relation between
`skos:Concept`s. The 17 default-build rows listed in `queue/items/B-147.yaml`
therefore imply a class/concept pun. B-106 already rejects that shape for
method vocabularies. Editorial intent in `alignment-main.ttl` was advisory,
and the existing research overlay's stronger axioms are still candidates.

## Detector and deliberate scope

`scripts/verify_mapping_policy.py`, already wired into `make test`, now tests
the published default import closure for all 12 predicates in the
`skos:semanticRelation` sub-property family. It checks an OWL class at either
end, without banning SKOS annotation predicates such as `skos:definition` on
classes. Its built-in self-test injects each predicate in both positions and
passes a concept-only control. Before the Turtle change, the command failed
with exactly 17 rows (exit 1). After the change, it passed (exit 0).

The research overlay has 11 further class/SKOS relations and case-study
modules 08/09 have 26 relative to the default class declarations. They are
outside this published-default detector and need a separate row-level
editorial review before the scope can expand. `knowledge/data/owl-skos-conventions-state.md`
records this limit and its retirement condition. That is remaining work,
not evidence that those overlays are class/concept-safe.
